Marry Me Chicken

24 June 2026  ·  5 servings  ·  60 min

pasta meal-prep

8/10
Overall
Taste
Effort

Ingredients

Main

  • 500 g shell pasta
  • 20 ml oil
  • 800 g chicken breast, boneless & skinless, diced
  • 400 g cottage cheese
  • 180 ml chicken stock
  • 50 g Parmigiano Reggiano, grated
  • 100 g sun-dried tomatoes
  • 200 g baby spinach, washed
  • 1 zucchini, diced
  • 3 bell peppers, sliced
  • Parmigiano Reggiano, to garnish

Spices

  • 1 tbsp dried Italian herbs
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • Salt & pepper

Steps

  1. Bring a pot of water to a boil, season generously with salt, and cook the pasta for 3 minutes less than the packet instructions. Drain and set aside.
  2. Add the chicken to a bowl and mix with the dried Italian herbs, salt, and pepper.
  3. In a blender, add the cottage cheese, chicken stock, onion powder, garlic powder, smoked paprika, Parmigiano Reggiano, sun-dried tomatoes, salt, and pepper. Blend on high for 2-3 minutes until smooth and no longer grainy.
  4. Place a large rimmed pan or pot over high heat. Add the sun-dried tomato oil and seasoned chicken. Cook for 4-5 minutes, mixing occasionally (work in batches if the pan isn't large enough).
  5. While the chicken is cooking, fry the zucchini and bell peppers in a separate pan until slightly softened and lightly browned.
  6. Turn the heat down to medium-low. Add the drained pasta to the chicken and pour over the cottage cheese mix. Add the fried zucchini and bell peppers, stir to combine, and cook for 1-2 minutes. Add the spinach and cook for 1 minute until wilted. Remove from the heat.
  7. Portion into 5 meal-prep containers. Garnish with Parmigiano Reggiano and cracked black pepper.

Notes

I made the Marry Me Chicken with extra zucchini and bell peppers. The prep was easy. It tastes really good and goes perfectly together. I could easily eat it every day.

Recipe adapted from Chef Jack Ovens
