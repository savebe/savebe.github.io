Spinach Zucchini Pasta with Salmon

30 June 2026  ·  5 servings  ·  40 min

pasta meal-prep salmon

3.5/10
Overall
Taste
Effort

Ingredients

Main

  • 1 kg zucchini, diced
  • 500 g pasta
  • 300 g baby spinach, washed
  • 200 ml vegetable stock
  • 200 g salmon fillet, diced
  • 200 g light cream cheese
  • 1 red onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • Olive oil

Spices

  • Salt
  • Pepper

Steps

  1. Cut the zucchini, red onion, and garlic. Wash the lemon, zest it, juice it, and cut the salmon into bite-sized pieces.
  2. Bring a pot of water to a boil, season generously with salt, and cook the pasta for 1-2 minutes less than the packet instructions. Reserve some pasta water, then drain and set aside.
  3. Place a large pan over medium-high heat. Add olive oil, the onion, and zucchini. Cook for 4-6 minutes, mixing occasionally, until the zucchini is slightly softened and lightly browned.
  4. Add the garlic and cook for 30-60 seconds, mixing frequently. Do not cook it for too long or it can turn bitter.
  5. Pour in the vegetable stock, then add the light cream cheese. Mix until smooth and creamy. Add a little lemon zest.
  6. Add the salmon to the sauce and cook over medium heat for 5-8 minutes, or until cooked through.
  7. Add the baby spinach in batches and cook until wilted.
  8. Add the cooked pasta and mix well to combine. Add pasta water until the sauce is glossy and creamy.
  9. Taste and adjust the seasoning with lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Start with half of the lemon juice and add more to taste.
  10. Portion into 5 meal-prep containers.

Notes

I had a lot of zucchini from the garden, so I added plenty of it to the pasta. It works really well with the creamy sauce.
