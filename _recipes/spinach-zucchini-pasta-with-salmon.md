ingredients:
  Main:
    - 1 kg zucchini, diced
    - 500 g pasta
    - 300 g baby spinach, washed
    - 200 ml vegetable stock
    - 200 g salmon fillet, diced
    - 200 g light cream cheese
    - 1 red onion, diced
    - 3 garlic cloves, minced
    - 1 lemon, zested and juiced
    - Olive oil
  Spices:
    - Salt
    - Pepper
steps:
  - Cut the zucchini, red onion, and garlic. Wash the lemon, zest it, juice it, and cut the salmon into bite-sized pieces.
  - Bring a pot of water to a boil, season generously with salt, and cook the pasta for 1-2 minutes less than the packet instructions. Reserve some pasta water, then drain and set aside.
  - Place a large pan over medium-high heat. Add olive oil, the onion, and zucchini. Cook for 4-6 minutes, mixing occasionally, until the zucchini is slightly softened and lightly browned.
  - Add the garlic and cook for 30-60 seconds, mixing frequently. Do not cook it for too long or it can turn bitter.
  - Pour in the vegetable stock, then add the light cream cheese. Mix until smooth and creamy. Add a little lemon zest.
  - Add the salmon to the sauce and cook over medium heat for 5-8 minutes, or until cooked through.
  - Add the baby spinach in batches and cook until wilted.
  - Add the cooked pasta and mix well to combine. Add pasta water until the sauce is glossy and creamy.
  - Taste and adjust the seasoning with lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Start with half of the lemon juice and add more to taste.
  - Portion into 5 meal-prep containers.

I had a lot of zucchini from the garden, so I added plenty of it to the pasta. It works really well with the creamy sauce.
